# EXP-3: PROMPT ENGINEERING

## Aim

Evaluation of 2024 Prompting Tools Across Diverse AI Platforms: ChatGPT, Claude, Bard (Gemini), Cohere Command, and Meta AI.

---

## Algorithm

### Step 1:

Select a common use case such as technical question answering and text summarization.

### Step 2:

Prepare the same prompt for all AI platforms.

### Step 3:

Submit the prompt to ChatGPT, Claude, Bard (Gemini), Cohere Command, and Meta AI.

### Step 4:

Collect the responses generated by each platform.

### Step 5:

Evaluate the responses based on:

* Accuracy
* Clarity
* Completeness
* Response Quality
* User Experience

### Step 6:

Compare the outputs and record observations.

### Step 7:

Analyze strengths and limitations of each platform.

### Step 8:

Draw conclusions based on the comparison.

---

## Prompt

**Technical Question:**
"Explain Artificial Intelligence and its applications in modern technology."

---

## Output

| AI Platform    | Response Quality | Accuracy | Detail Level | User Experience                     |
| -------------- | ---------------- | -------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| ChatGPT        | Excellent        | High     | High         | Easy to use and detailed            |
| Claude         | Excellent        | High     | High         | Very clear and well-structured      |
| Bard (Gemini)  | Very Good        | High     | Medium       | Fast and informative                |
| Cohere Command | Good             | Medium   | Medium       | Simple and concise                  |
| Meta AI        | Good             | Medium   | Medium       | Quick response and easy interaction |

### Observations

* **ChatGPT** provided detailed explanations with examples and structured content.
* **Claude** produced clear, well-organized, and context-rich responses.
* **Bard (Gemini)** generated informative responses with good factual accuracy.
* **Cohere Command** gave concise answers suitable for quick understanding.
* **Meta AI** offered fast responses but with comparatively less detail.

---

## Result

The evaluation of prompting tools across ChatGPT, Claude, Bard (Gemini), Cohere Command, and Meta AI was successfully completed. Among the platforms tested, ChatGPT and Claude produced the most comprehensive and accurate responses, while Bard (Gemini), Cohere Command, and Meta AI provided useful but comparatively less detailed outputs. Hence, prompt effectiveness and response quality vary across AI platforms depending on their design and capabilities.
